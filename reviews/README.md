# Reviews

Reviews capture engineering findings and verdicts. They should focus on risks,
evidence, and actions rather than personal preference.

Use review records when a change is significant enough that future maintainers
should understand what was checked and what risks remained.

Recommended filename format:

```text
YYYY-MM-DD-short-name-review.md
```

See `../examples/reviews/example-review.md` for the expected format.
